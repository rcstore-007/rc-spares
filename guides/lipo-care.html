LiPo battery safety and storage

How to charge, store and get rid of LiPo batteries safely so they last longer.

Step by step

  1. Always use a balance charger (a charger that fills each cell evenly) on the LiPo setting.
  2. Charge at 1C: a 2200 mAh battery at 2.2A, a 5000 mAh battery at 5A.
  3. Never charge when nobody is around, and never charge on a bed, sofa or carpet.
  4. If you won't use it for more than a day or two, use storage mode (about 3.80 to 3.85V per cell).
  5. Never run a cell below 3.0V. Set the speed controller's cutoff to LiPo.
  6. Keep batteries in a fireproof LiPo bag or a metal box, in a cool room.

Careful: A puffed (swollen) battery should never be charged, pressed or punctured. Tape the plug, keep it away from anything that burns, and take it to an e-waste or battery collection point.

Good to know: A LiPo kept at storage voltage lasts hundreds of charges. One stored full or run flat often lasts under a hundred.

Common questions

What should I do with a puffed LiPo battery?

Stop using it. Don't charge, press or puncture it; tape the plug and take it to an e-waste or battery collection point.

What voltage should I store a LiPo at?

About 3.80–3.85V per cell. Most balance chargers have a storage mode that does this automatically.

Is my cheap balance charger genuine?

Copies of popular chargers are common and some overcharge or lack proper cooling. Buy from a trusted seller and check the maker's authenticity code where offered.
