Problems

Body shell cracking or paint peeling

The polycarbonate body splits at the corners or mounting holes, or the paint flakes off.

Why it happens

  • Thin polycarbonate (Lexan) splits at corners and body-post holes after repeated impacts, especially in cold weather.
  • Paint flakes when the shell wasn't washed before painting, or when the wrong paint type was used.

Check these, in order

  1. Wash the inside of the shell with warm soapy water to remove mould-release oil before any repair or paint.
  2. Reinforce high-stress areas from the inside with self-adhesive fibreglass mesh tape, then a thin layer of flexible shoe-repair adhesive over it.
  3. Stitch existing cracks with small zip ties through drilled holes, then cover with the same adhesive.
  4. Paint only with polycarbonate paint, sprayed on the inside of the shell, darkest colours first.
  5. Let paint cure 24–48 hours before adding decals or reinforcement.

Careful: Ordinary hobby or acrylic paint isn't flexible enough for a polycarbonate shell and will crack off at the first impact.

Good to know: Reinforce a new body before its first run. It's far easier than repairing it later.

Common questions

How do I stop my RC body from cracking?

Reinforce the inside with fibreglass mesh tape and a flexible adhesive at the corners and post holes, before the first run.

Why is the paint peeling off my RC body?

Either the mould-release oil wasn't washed off, or non-polycarbonate paint was used. Polycarbonate paint, sprayed inside the shell, flexes with it.
