Problems

Stuck on the trail

Your crawler can't get over an obstacle, tips over on climbs, or the winch struggles.

Why it happens

  • Weight sits too high or too far back, so the car tips on climbs.
  • Tyres or inserts are too hard to grip the rock.
  • A winch powered from the receiver doesn't get enough power under load.

Check these, in order

  1. Drive slowly. Most crawler climbs are won with steady, low throttle.
  2. Add weight low and to the front: brass wheels, hubs or steering cups.
  3. Fit softer tyres or inserts so the tyre wraps around the rock.
  4. For a heavy pull, double the winch line with a snatch block (a small pulley hooked to the car), which halves the load on the winch.
  5. Power the winch straight from the battery or from a separate BEC, not only from the receiver.
  6. If the winch stops and blinks, its overload protection has switched on. Let it cool for a few minutes.

Good to know: A cheap bumper winch looks great, but for real pulling a stronger winch with metal gears lasts much longer.

Common questions

Why does my crawler lift a front wheel when climbing?

That's torque twist: the driveshaft's reaction twists the chassis. Stiffer springs or thicker oil on the rear corner that compresses, and weight on the lifting wheel, reduce it.

Bumper winch or internal servo winch?

A bumper winch looks the part but small ones strip under heavy pulls. A servo winch mounted inside the chassis is much stronger for real recovery.

How do I add weight to my RC crawler?

Use brass wheels, hubs, steering cups or knuckles: low down and towards the front. Avoid weight high up on the body, which makes the car tip.
