Care

Driving in Indian summer heat

At 40°C and above, parts overheat much sooner. Simple changes keep the car running.

Step by step

  1. Drive early in the morning or in the evening, not in the afternoon sun.
  2. Fit a pinion one or two teeth smaller than in winter.
  3. Add a cooling fan to the motor and keep air flowing through the body.
  4. Use slightly thicker shock oil, because heat thins it.
  5. Rest the car between batteries and let the motor cool.
  6. Keep LiPo batteries indoors in a cool room. Never leave them in a parked car or in the sun.

Careful: Don't charge a LiPo that is still hot from driving. Let it cool down first.

Common questions

Is it okay to run my RC car in 40°C heat?

Yes, with care: gear down a tooth or two, add a motor fan, run in the morning or evening, and let things cool between packs.
