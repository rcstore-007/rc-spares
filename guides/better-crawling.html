Better crawling

Upgrades that make a scale crawler climb better, in the order that helps most.

Step by step

  1. Soft tyres with good inserts. This is the single biggest improvement.
  2. Weight low and at the front: brass wheels, hubs or steering cups.
  3. A strong, metal-gear steering servo, so the wheels turn even when pressed into rocks.
  4. Slightly thicker shock oil, so the car settles smoothly instead of bouncing.
  5. A motor and ESC with good low-speed control and drag brake (holds the car still on slopes when you let go of the throttle).

Good to know: Practice slow, steady throttle. Driving style matters as much as parts on the rocks.

Common questions

What should I upgrade first on an SCX24?

A metal-gear servo, sticky tyres and a little brass weight low at the front. Then steel worm gears if you upgrade the motor.

What are the common TRX-4M problems?

Owners often report drag from plastic bushings, a weak stock servo and plastic diff gears. A sealed bearing kit is usually the first fix, then a metal-gear servo.
