Problems

Battery runs out too fast

The car slows down or stops after only a few minutes.

Why it happens

  • The battery is old. LiPo batteries (the usual flat rechargeable packs) wear out after a few hundred charges.
  • The LVC (low-voltage cutoff, a safety feature in the speed controller that stops the car when the battery gets low) is switching off early because the battery voltage drops under load.
  • Something is dragging, like a stiff bearing or gears that are too tight.
  • Dirty or loose battery plugs.

Check these, in order

  1. Charge the battery fully and check each cell (each section inside the battery) with a cell checker. A full cell reads 4.20V, and all cells should read almost the same.
  2. Spin each wheel by hand with the battery unplugged. They should spin freely. Gritty or stiff wheels mean the bearings (small metal rings the wheel spins on) need cleaning or replacing.
  3. Clean the battery and speed controller plugs with isopropyl alcohol and a cotton bud.
  4. Check the speed controller is set to the right battery type (LiPo or NiMH). The wrong setting can cut power early.
  5. Charge with a balance charger at 1C (the battery's capacity in amps: a 2200 mAh battery charges at 2.2A).

Careful: If a LiPo battery is puffed up (swollen), stop using it. Don't charge it, press it or puncture it. Never charge a LiPo when nobody is around. If one catches fire, use sand, not water.

Good to know: If you won't use a LiPo for more than a day or two, use the charger's storage mode to keep it at about 3.80 to 3.85V per cell. Stored full, it wears out much faster.

Common questions

Why does my RC car stop after a few minutes?

Usually the low-voltage cutoff is tripping because an ageing or low-C battery sags under load. Check each cell's voltage, spin the wheels for drag, and clean the plugs.

How long should an RC LiPo battery last?

Looked after properly and stored at storage voltage, a good pack lasts a few hundred charge cycles. Stored full or run flat, it can wear out in under a hundred.

Is my LiPo battery dead if it won't charge?

If the charger shows low voltage, a cell has dropped below about 3.0V. Packs discharged that far are not worth reviving; replace the battery.

Smart LiPo or regular LiPo?

Smart packs add convenience features but cost more and need matching chargers. A regular LiPo with a standard balance lead works with any balance charger and is usually the better value.
