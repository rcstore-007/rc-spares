Store policies

How ordering, delivery, returns and privacy work at RC Spares. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Ordering and paymentShipping and deliveryReturns, refunds and cancellationsWarrantyPrivacyContact and complaints

Ordering and payment

Add parts to your cart and send the order to us on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm availability, the final price and the shipping cost before you pay. Nothing is charged on this website.

Payment methods: UPI or bank transfer. Prices are in Indian rupees (₹).

Shipping and delivery

We deliver across India. Orders usually ship within 14–21 days after payment is confirmed. The shipping cost is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay, and we share tracking details once your order is sent.

Returns, refunds and cancellations

If a part arrives damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos or an unboxing video. After checking, we will replace the part or refund you. Parts that have been installed, used, modified or damaged by wrong use cannot be returned.

You can cancel an order for a full refund any time before it is shipped. Approved refunds are paid back to the way you paid.

Warranty

Parts are sold without any warranty. If a part arrives damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered, the returns policy above applies.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to deliver your order: your name, phone number and delivery address, which you share with us on WhatsApp. We use them to confirm, ship and support your order, and share them only with the courier delivering it. We do not sell your details.

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or ads. Your cart is saved only in your own browser so it is still there when you come back; you can clear it at any time.

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you by messaging +91 9346631998.
