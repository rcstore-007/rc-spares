Where to start upgrading

For beginners: upgrade in this order and you'll spend less and break less. Reliability first, power last.

First

The foundation

Small spends that improve every run, before any power upgrade.

  • Balance charger and a spare pack
  • Proper hex drivers (1.5 / 2.0 / 2.5 mm)
  • Tyres matched to your terrain
  • Metal-gear servo with enough torque
Batteries & chargers · Tyres · Servos · Tools
Next

Reliability

Then upgrade whatever breaks first. Keep plastic arms and bumpers: they flex and protect costlier parts.

  • Rubber-sealed (2RS) bearings
  • Metal driveshafts
  • Hardened steel gears
  • Fresh shock oil and seals
Gears & drivetrain · Shocks
Then

Power

Only once the drivetrain holds. Start on 2S and gear down when you add cells.

  • Matched brushless motor + ESC combo
  • 2S, then 3S LiPo packs
  • Pinion one or two teeth smaller per step up
  • Motor cooling fan
