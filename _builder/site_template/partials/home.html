Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply as soon as we can.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are delivered in {{DAYS}} days after payment is confirmed. Some arrive sooner, depending on where you are.

How do I place an order?

Add parts to your cart and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". Your order reaches us as a message and we reply to confirm it.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order and the shipping cost on WhatsApp, we share the payment details. We dispatch after payment is received.

How much is shipping?

It depends on your location and the size of the order. We tell you the exact amount on WhatsApp before you pay.

How do I know which scale my car is?

The scale (for example 1:10 or 1:8) is printed on the box and in the manual. You can also send us your car's name or a photo on WhatsApp.

How do I know a part fits my car?

Each part lists the models and scale it fits. If you're unsure, add your car's name in the order note and we will check before confirming.

What if a part is wrong or damaged?

Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the part and packaging, and we'll arrange a replacement or return.

Can you get a part that isn't listed?

Yes, we source parts on request. Send us the part name, your car's model and scale, or a photo of the old part. We reply with the price and delivery time before you pay anything.
