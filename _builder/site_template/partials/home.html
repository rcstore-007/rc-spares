Where to start upgrading

For beginners: upgrade in this order and you'll spend less and break less. Reliability first, power last.

First

The foundation

A balance charger and a spare pack, proper hex drivers, tyres matched to your terrain, and a metal-gear servo with enough torque for them. Small spends that improve every run.

Batteries & chargers · Tyres · Servos · Tools
Next

Reliability

Sealed bearings, metal driveshafts and hardened gears, then upgrade whatever breaks first. Keep plastic suspension arms and bumpers: they flex on impact and protect the costlier parts behind them.

Gears & drivetrain · Shocks
Then

Power

A matched brushless motor and ESC. Run it on 2S first, step up only once the drivetrain holds, and drop a tooth or two on the pinion whenever you add cells.
